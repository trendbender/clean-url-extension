Clean URL helps you remove tracking parameters from links before sharing.

✔ Remove UTM tags (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term)
✔ Remove ad click IDs (gclid, fbclid, msclkid, and more)
✔ Remove shopping and email/marketing IDs (srsltid, mc_*, mkt_tok)
✔ Remove referral and tracking parameters (ref, igshid, and 50+ more)
✔ Smart per-site cleanup for major search, social, video and shopping sites — removes their tracking without breaking the link
✔ Strip "scroll-to-text" fragments (#:~:text=)
✔ Works fully locally — no data collection, no analytics
✔ One click. No login. No noise.

How it works:
1. Open any page
2. Click the Clean URL icon — or right-click → Copy Clean URL
3. Copy a clean, shareable link instantly

You stay in control:
• Toggle each cleaning rule on/off — your choice is remembered
• See the original and cleaned URL side by side
• Copy Clean, Copy Original, or Open Clean

Available in 7 languages — the interface follows your browser language automatically.

Clean URL works entirely in your browser. No tracking. No external servers.
